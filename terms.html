Last updated 22 September 2026

MetaSelf is a personal Android application, written by one person for their own use. It is not sold, not advertised, not published on any app store, and is installed by copying a file. These terms are short because the arrangement is simple.

It is not medical advice

Nothing in this application is medical, dietary or clinical advice. It computes a daily calorie target from population formulas — the same ones every such app uses — which carry real error bars for any individual. It estimates the contents of meals using an AI model, and reads nutrition figures from a community-maintained food database. Both are often approximately right and sometimes wrong.

If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are recovering from an eating disorder, or are making any decision where being wrong would matter, speak to a qualified professional. Do not use these numbers in place of one.

The numbers are estimates, and say so

Every figure the application stores records where it came from — typed by you, estimated by a model, read off a package label, or copied from an earlier meal. This is deliberate, so that a guess can never quietly be mistaken for a measurement. Please treat them accordingly.

Keep your own copies. The application can export everything you have logged to a single file at any time, and can write a copy automatically to a folder you choose. Use them.

Services it uses on your behalf

  • OpenAI — meal descriptions are sent for estimation using your own API key. Their terms and pricing apply to you directly, and any cost is yours.
  • Open Food Facts — barcodes are looked up in their open database, and products you choose to contribute are sent under your own account. Their terms apply.
  • Google Drive — if you enable it, backups are written to your own Drive. Google's terms apply.

The application has no service of its own, no server, and no account. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to cancel.

If you contribute food data

Sending a product to Open Food Facts publishes it, credited to your username, under that database's licence. Send only what is printed on the package. The application will not send an AI estimate to a shared database, because a guess published where others rely on it is a different thing from a guess kept to yourself.
