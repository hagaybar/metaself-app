# The face reaches every screen — the rest of issue #14

**Goal:** finish public issue #14 ("Give the app a face"). Step one of D48 (0.34.0) gave the app its
typefaces, type scale and ink ladder, and D49–D52 relaid the day and the record. What is left is the
part of D48 that is a rule about *screens* rather than about the theme: screens use `Spacing` instead
of the frame's one 16 dp gap for everything, red means only a refusal or a field that is wrong, small
print sits on a named step of the ladder, and the two lists that still spell out "Edit" and "Delete"
on every row get compact actions.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-21-the-page-design.md`, D48. No decision changes. No new
dependency: `Icons.Outlined.Edit` and `Icons.Outlined.Delete` are in `material-icons-core`, which
`material3` already brings in (it is where the app's `Icons.Filled.Add` comes from).

**Before every Gradle command:** `export ANDROID_HOME=/home/ubuntu/android-sdk`; use
`~/bin/gradlew-safe`; redirect output to a file and check `$?`; stage by explicit path.

## Settled choices (from the brief)

- Row actions become `IconButton`s (48 dp targets by construction) with outlined pencil / bin icons,
  each with a spoken name that includes the item's name: "Edit Yoghurt", "Delete Yoghurt"; for a
  weight reading, the day it is for. This also advances public issue #3 for those rows. Delete keeps
  today's behaviour (entries: delete at once with Undo; readings: delete with Undo).
- Grouping uses the four existing tokens only.
- Red only for refusals and field errors. Teal only where a decision already puts it.

## Choices D48 does not settle (made here, most consistent with the day page)

1. **The icons are drawn in Ink three**, the caption step, including the bin: the day page draws no
   control in red, and "Delete" is not an error.
2. **Warnings about a food's data** (cannot be costed; facts disagree) move from red to **Ink** — full
   strength, one step above the caption around them. They are facts about the record in the voice of
   every other fact, as over-target became; they still outrank the small print they sit in.
3. **The restore question** becomes a `surfaceVariant` card with Ink text, the app's ordinary card,
   instead of an error-red container. It is a question before a destructive act, like the foods and
   meals "Delete …? This cannot be undone.", which is already drawn in ink.
4. **Padding inside a row or a card** (6, 10, 12 dp) is left alone: it sets touch height, not the gap
   between things, and none of those figures is a D48 token.
