# The app answers the finger — public issue #16

**Goal:** public issue #16 ("Nothing in the app answers the finger: no motion, no haptics"). Its
rule, from the design committee: **today moves, the past is still.**

**Spec:** no decision changes. D49 removed the ring, so "the ring animates to its value" is applied
to what the day draws now: the headline figure and the hairline under it. D14 ("the past is never
coloured and never nags") is the reason the past is also still. No new dependency: `AnimatedContent`
and `animateFloatAsState` are in `androidx.compose.animation`, already on the classpath through
`material3`/`foundation`; `LocalHapticFeedback` is in `compose-ui`.

**Before every Gradle command:** `export ANDROID_HOME=/home/ubuntu/android-sdk`; use
`~/bin/gradlew-safe`; redirect output to a file and check `$?`; stage by explicit path.

Haptics and permissions: `LocalHapticFeedback` in compose-ui 1.6.1 has exactly two types. `LongPress`
calls `View.performHapticFeedback(LONG_PRESS)`; `TextHandleMove` calls
`View.performHapticFeedback(9)` (`TEXT_HANDLE_MOVE`, API 27+). `View.performHapticFeedback` needs no
`VIBRATE` permission and obeys the system's own touch-feedback setting. On API 26 the light tick is a
no-op (unknown constant); the thump still works.

## Design

- **`Motion`** (pure, `ui/theme/Motion.kt`): `systemAllows(animatorScale)` — false at 0 (the system
  "Remove animations" switch sets `ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE` to 0), and false for a nonsense value;
  `moves(isToday, systemAllows)`. Durations: press 100 ms, settle 300 ms; easing
  `FastOutSlowInEasing` (Material standard). No spring physics on data.
- **`LocalMoves`**: a composition local, default **false** (still). `MainActivity` provides the system
  answer, re-read on every resume. The day page and the record screen narrow it by `isToday`, so
  everything drawn for a past day is still without each component knowing about days.
- **Press spring**: `Modifier.springsWhenPressed(source)` scales to 0.98 while pressed, only when
  `LocalMoves`. Applied to the rows that are doors or can be chosen: the day's part rows, the
  three ways in, record rows, a built meal's row, food rows. The ripple stays as it is.
- **Thump**: `HapticFeedbackType.LongPress` on every long press that starts or extends a choice.
- **Tick**: `HapticFeedbackType.TextHandleMove` on every tap that ticks or unticks a row while
  choosing. Haptics are not motion, so they are not gated by day or by the animation setting.
- **Day's answer**: the figure rolls (old slides up and out, new slides up in, with a fade, 300 ms)
  when it changes on today; the rule fills from empty to its value over 300 ms on arrival and eases
  to a new value. The macro and step rules share `ProportionRule` and so do the same. Past days and
  "Remove animations": both snap, exactly as now.

## Steps (one commit each)

1. Plan (this file).
2. `Motion` + tests (JUnit 5): the decision table.
3. Haptics on long press and tick, record rows and food rows. Render tests (JUnit 4,
   `createComposeRule`) with a recording `HapticFeedback` provided through `LocalHapticFeedback`:
   they prove the app **asks** for the right haptic at the right moment. Nothing here can prove the
   phone vibrates.
4. Motion: `LocalMoves` from the system setting, narrowed by day; figure roll; rule fill; press
   spring. Render test with the clock paused: on today, mid-roll, both the old and new figure are
   in the tree; on a past day, and with motion off, only the new one. Robolectric cannot see the
   rule's width, a scale, or timing — those are phone checks.
5. Full suite + lint.

## Not in this plan

- The checkmark's stroke on a **past** day's record. It is the stock `Checkbox`'s own 100 ms draw;
  stopping it per day would mean replacing the control and its toggle semantics. It does obey
  "Remove animations".
- Springs on every other button in the app (dialogs, bars, forms). They keep the ripple.
- The pager's own slide and the pull-to-refresh indicator: those follow the finger, not the data.
